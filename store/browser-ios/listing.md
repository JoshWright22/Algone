## Full description

Algone Browser is a small browser for 12 social and video websites. It loads their mobile websites and hides the algorithmic parts, then gives you a home of your own: search, a saved queue, and timed unlocks.

Works in the browser only. This app is the browser: Algone applies to pages you open inside it. It does not change native apps, so the YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok apps stay as they are. Uninstalling always works.

Sites: YouTube, Reddit, X, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Threads, Bluesky, Twitch, Pinterest, and Substack. Sites open from plain text tiles. There is no free address bar, and other links open in your system browser.

- Search-first home, with an optional question about what you came to do.
- Subscriptions first where the site supports it.
- A local queue, filled by a save button and the share sheet. Stops at the end of the queue.
- A launch pause, with a one tap way out.
- The same modes and barriers as the Algone extension, with every barrier screen in the app.

Limits you should know: YouTube should be expected to work logged out, because Google blocks sign-in in embedded web views. Other sites may also challenge logins. There is no ad filter in this app. If filtering cannot load, the site shows unfiltered and the app tells you.

Your settings stay on your device. There is no account and no data collection. Once a day the app downloads updated site rules, which are data and not code.

Algone Browser has no link with the sites it opens. All names belong to their owners. Open source under the MIT license: https://github.com/JoshWright22/Algone

## Limits to state plainly

- Works in the browser only.
- Does not change native apps. The YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok apps stay as they are.
- Uninstalling always works.
- Sites change their pages, so a rule can stop working until it is repaired.
- Some signed-in pages are not checked yet. The site status page lists each one.
